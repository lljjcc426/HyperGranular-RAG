```text

Comparing that array to scalar `11` yielded a nonempty boolean array, which the `if` condition treated as true. The fail-closed stop was therefore caused by a validation-wrapper collection-cardinality expression defect. This diagnosis does not authorize replacing the expression with `@(...).Count` and rerunning the gate.
