# JIIS bounded empirical-study retarget

Main public reading copy: `Manuscript_JIIS_Neutral.pdf` (22 pages). This is a venue-directed empirical manuscript, not a new experiment or a claim of acceptance.

The story follows learned set discrimination → actually selected evidence → paired reader outcomes, with exact-search fidelity and measured cost as a secondary question. All numerical results and limitations retain their prior study identities.

Read `FINAL_QC.md`, `REVIEW_AND_REVISION_LOG.md`, `OFFICIAL_REQUIREMENTS.md` and `SUBMISSION_FILE_MAP.md`. Public numerical resources contain de-identified existing saved scores only. Full author-bearing submission files and source ZIP are local under `submission_local/` and intentionally excluded from Git.

The analysis scripts are editorial build/packaging utilities. `convert_layout.py` records the initial conversion and must not be rerun over the final edited main.tex. `prepare_private.py` requires the previously confirmed local author metadata. Nothing here launches model experiments.
