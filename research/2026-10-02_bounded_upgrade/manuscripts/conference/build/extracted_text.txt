758         Scoring and interpretation.  The reanalysis fixes
759          the original predictions and reference channel. It

763         only where the dataset permits aliases.   Hot-
764       potQA assigns zero token F1 to mismatched
765       yes/no/noanswer responses, and zero F1 when
766         both normalized strings are empty. MuSiQue’s of-
